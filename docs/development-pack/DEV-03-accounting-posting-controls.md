## Non-negotiable invariants
Posted journals/lines and original AccountId are immutable; corrections are separate linked reversals/adjustments, with closed-period control. A unique stable source business event identity and payload hash prevent duplicate publication even if a document version changes. Validate decimal balanced base-currency journal, authoritative company and period, independent maker-checker, allowed account, original money/rate and inventory cost. Stock issue, invoicing, delivery and payment are distinct signed recognition events.

## Posting and operational controls
B.3 Journal posting contract and operational posting matrix
GL-01: Document states: Draft → PendingApproval (conditional) → Approved → Posted; rejected draft returns to Draft. Posted transactions and their journal lines are immutable. Correction requires a reversing or adjusting journal linked to the original; no physical delete or silent unposting. Closed periods block normal changes, including reversal dated into the closed period.
GL-02: Posting is atomic and idempotent per stable business event identity (tenant + company + source system + event type + immutable source event ID), independent of document revision. A retry with the same canonical payload returns its original posting reference; the same event identity with a changed payload is a conflict requiring authorized correction or new reversal/adjustment event. Document/version is retained for optimistic concurrency and audit, not as a way to create a second posting. Validate company/period/account activation, permissions, amounts, exchange rates and balanced functional-currency debits/credits in a single database transaction. Unique source-event keys prevent duplicate POS/ERP/API imports and retries. Store original currency, foreign amount, approved rate, rate date, functional amount and rounding difference.
GL-03: Posting matrix (illustrative, excluding taxes/discounts): cash sale on recognition: Dr Cash/Undeposited Funds, Cr Revenue; delivered stock: Dr Cost of Goods Sold, Cr Inventory. Credit sale: Dr Accounts Receivable, Cr Revenue; collection: Dr Bank, Cr Accounts Receivable. Supplier invoice: Dr Expense/Inventory/GRNI, Cr Accounts Payable; supplier payment: Dr Accounts Payable, Cr Bank. Inventory count shortage: Dr Approved Stock Adjustment Expense, Cr Inventory; positive adjustment: Dr Inventory, Cr Approved Adjustment Offset. Each debit/credit pair must be defined for returns, credit notes, expenses, fixed assets, payroll imports and FX differences before enabling its transaction type.
GL-04: Goods received before supplier billing use a configured Goods Received Not Invoiced (GRNI)/accrual policy. Three-way match tolerances and variance posting are configurable and approval-controlled. Source-document reversals must reverse the applicable recognition and settlement events without duplicate postings.
GL-05: KWD uses three-decimal currency display/accounting precision unless a required rule specifies otherwise; keep internal rates with sufficient precision and make round-off allocation explicit. Functional currency balances must reconcile. OPEN: approve permitted mixed-currency journal rules, default rate source, gains/loss account and tolerance.
B.4 AR, AP, sales and purchase document lifecycles
ARAP-01: Sales invoice lifecycle: Draft → Approved/Issued → PartiallyPaid → Paid, with distinct CreditAdjusted, VoidedBeforePosting or ReversedAfterPosting paths. Receipt allocation supports partial, split, advance/unapplied, overpayment, refund and write-off with permission and reason. AR subledger must reconcile to GL control accounts.
ARAP-02: Purchase lifecycle separates Purchase Order, Goods Receipt, Supplier Bill, Credit Note and Payment. Duplicate supplier invoice detection uses supplier, company, invoice reference and dates plus controlled override. AP supports split/partial allocations, advances, credit adjustments, refunds and payment cancellation by reversal; AP subledger reconciles to GL.
ARAP-03: Quotes, orders, delivery notes and invoices are distinct business events; issuing a quote/order does not itself recognize income unless an approved accounting policy explicitly says so. Tax and discount treatment comes from jurisdiction and transaction-specific configuration, never hard-coded Kuwait VAT assumptions.
B.5 Inventory valuation, POS and integrations
INV-01: Company selects an approved valuation policy (perpetual weighted-average or FIFO where supported) at activation. Each stock movement records item, warehouse, unit, quantity, rate/cost layer, source and time. Policy changes require effective date, approval, explicit migration/revaluation and period lock checks. Physical stock quantity and inventory value must reconcile to their ledgers.
INV-02: Negative stock is blocked by default for valued items; any approved exception must explicitly define cost estimate, later correction, reporting and reconciliation. Backdated receipts/issues must trigger reliable valuation recalculation or be blocked by period policy. Returns use the original cost layer or defined fallback with auditable variance.
INV-03: POS import includes event ID, receipt/refund ID, branch, date, line taxes/discounts, tender and stock cost data. Post once from a clearly designated system-of-record. Duplicate source events are rejected or safely recognized as retries. A stock movement and its COGS must not be posted twice via POS and Inventory integrations.
B.6 Reconciliation, closing and financial statements
CLOSE-01: Pre-close checklist verifies unposted drafts, GL debit/credit balance, AR/AP-to-control-GL reconciliation, bank reconciliation, cash counts, stock-to-GL value, open GRNI, suspense, depreciation, accruals, prepayments, FX revaluation and exceptions. Unresolved blocking items prevent period close; explicit exceptions require approved policy.
CLOSE-02: Financial statement outputs are generated from approved posted ledger balances and a versioned statement-line mapping; each statement line drills down to account/journal/source. Cash-flow classification and changes-in-equity movement rules are separately documented. Reports must tie: Assets = Liabilities + Equity; statement movements agree to trial balance and retained earnings.
CLOSE-03: Freeze each approved statement as an immutable version with company, scope, currency, reporting period, source-ledger cutoff, mappings, preparer, approver and approval time. Closing locks transactions as of the period; reopening must capture justification, separate approval, all affected reports/filings and downstream reapproval requirements.

## Default policy matrix, not approved accounting advice
B.15 Required event-to-journal posting matrix (configuration baseline)
GLM-00: The following entries are default event contracts, not unconditional tax or IFRS accounting advice. An approved accounting-policy matrix must specify every transaction type's recognition trigger, debit/credit control accounts, amount basis, inventory-cost basis, tax lines, timing, allowed reversals, approval thresholds and evidence. A source event must be mapped to exactly one posting policy and processed idempotently. Never enable a source transaction with no approved posting policy.
GLM-01 | Cash/counter sale recognized and paid: Dr Cash/Bank/Undeposited Funds; Cr Sales Revenue and applicable tax liability per configured tax rules. For delivered valued goods: Dr COGS; Cr Inventory at the approved issue cost. Tender split must reconcile to invoice consideration.
GLM-02 | Credit sales invoice recognized: Dr Trade Receivables; Cr Revenue and any applicable tax liability. Inventory/COGS journal posts at the approved goods-delivery recognition event, not automatically at quotation or sales order creation.
GLM-03 | Customer cash receipt against invoice: Dr Cash/Bank; Cr Trade Receivables for allocated amount. Partial/unapplied amount remains traceable; a customer advance before earned/invoiced sale generally credits Customer Advances liability under approved policy.
GLM-04 | Apply an approved customer advance to an invoice: Dr Customer Advances liability; Cr Trade Receivables. Preserve advance source, allocation date, refund rights and exchange differences if applicable.
GLM-05 | Sales credit note/return: Dr Sales Returns or contra-revenue and applicable tax liability reversal; Cr Trade Receivables or refund payable per settlement state. If restocked and eligible, Dr Inventory; Cr COGS at original eligible cost; damaged/non-restockable returns follow separate write-off policy.
GLM-06 | Purchase order approval: no GL posting by itself. On valued stock receipt under perpetual GRNI policy: Dr Inventory; Cr Goods Received Not Invoiced (GRNI), including landed costs where applicable. No duplicate receipt posting on invoice match.
GLM-07 | Supplier invoice for stock previously accrued to GRNI: Dr GRNI for matched received value; Dr/Cr inventory or purchase variance under approved costing rules; Dr applicable recoverable input tax if legally permitted; Cr Trade Payables for gross supplier liability. Non-stock expense bill: Dr Expense/Asset and any permitted recoverable input tax; Cr Trade Payables for total payable. Non-recoverable tax forms part of expense/asset cost under the approved tax and accounting policy. No tax treatment is assumed by default.
GLM-08 | Supplier payment: Dr Trade Payables; Cr Bank/Cash for allocated amount. Supplier advance made before invoice: Dr Supplier Advances asset; Cr Bank; later approved allocation: Dr Trade Payables; Cr Supplier Advances.
GLM-09 | Inventory shortage/adjustment: Dr Authorized Inventory Loss/COGS adjustment; Cr Inventory. Surplus: Dr Inventory; Cr approved Inventory Adjustment Gain/Offset. Every count variance retains stock-count evidence, valuation and independent approval.
GLM-10 | Cash-to-bank transfer: Dr Destination Bank; Cr Source Cash/Bank, with in-transit clearing account when dates differ. Internal transfers never recognize sales revenue.
GLM-11 | Fixed asset capitalization: Dr Fixed Asset; Cr Bank/Trade Payables at approved cost; periodic depreciation: Dr Depreciation Expense; Cr Accumulated Depreciation. Disposals clear historical cost and accumulated depreciation and record resulting gain/loss with approval.
GLM-12 | Expense accrual/prepayment: Dr Expense and Cr Accrued Liability for accrued unpaid cost; prepaid expense initially Dr Prepaid Asset and Cr Bank/AP then systematically amortize Dr Expense and Cr Prepaid Asset. Reversals and cut-off follow approved close policy.
GLM-13 | FX settlement or revaluation: retain original currency/rate and base-currency monetary revaluation; difference posts to configured realized/unrealized FX gain/loss accounts. No source-currency arithmetic balance assertion is forced on legitimately mixed-currency journals.
GLM-14 | Opening balances and year-end carry forward: import opening subledger balances with source evidence and a balanced opening journal; approved retained earnings transfer/closing entries must tie to profit and loss and statement of changes in equity. Avoid duplicate year-end carry-forward entries.
GLM-15 | Integration integrity: if Sales, POS, Inventory or AP provides overlapping operational events, designate the system of record and exactly one journal publisher per source event. On retry or cancellation, reconcile original posting ID, version, business reference and reversal link.

## Event ledger scenarios
F.3 Authoritative accounting posting/event matrix – policy-controlled
PM-01 Cash sale on approved recognition event: Dr Cash/Undeposited Funds, Cr Revenue; tax liability line only where legally applicable; if perpetual inventory goods are delivered, separately Dr COGS, Cr Inventory exactly once.
PM-02 Credit sale on approved recognition event: Dr AR, Cr Revenue (plus applicable tax liability); receipt allocation: Dr Bank/Cash, Cr AR. Unapplied advance: Dr Bank/Cash, Cr Customer Advance Liability until applied under approved policy.
PM-03 Supplier bill for expensed goods/services: Dr Expense (or accrued liability/GRNI clearing according to receipt policy), Cr AP; settlement: Dr AP, Cr Bank. Goods receipt for perpetual stock before invoice: Dr Inventory, Cr GRNI; supplier bill clears GRNI and posts price variances by approved policy.
PM-04 Sales credit note/return: reverse relevant revenue, tax and receivable/cash obligation; inventory return, if physically received and resalable, Dr Inventory, Cr COGS at original auditable cost policy. Refund and credit note must not double reverse the same event.
PM-05 Purchase return/credit: reverse AP/expense or inventory/GRNI as applicable to original recognition; handle supplier refund/credit allocation separately. No reversal into locked period.
PM-06 Inventory shortage: Dr approved stock loss/adjustment expense, Cr Inventory; surplus: Dr Inventory, Cr approved gain/adjustment account. Transfers within same legal company shift location/value layers without recognizing sales revenue; cross-company movement requires separate intercompany legal/accounting flow.
PM-07 Fixed asset purchase/capitalization: Dr Fixed Asset, Cr AP/Bank under capitalization policy; periodic depreciation: Dr Depreciation Expense, Cr Accumulated Depreciation. Disposal requires approved gain/loss and accumulated depreciation reversal rules before enabling.
PM-08 FX settlement difference: post realized FX gain/loss to approved account; period-end revaluation uses separate unrealized FX policy and reversing/settlement rules. Exchange-rate source, date and rounding are auditable.
PM-09 Payroll import: approved net pay, employee liabilities, employer expense and statutory deductions are mapped from the authoritative HRMS payroll journal. Import is idempotent; do not recreate payroll calculation inside accounting unless separately scoped.
PM-10 Payment refund, bank transfer, discount, write-off, advance application and adjustment each require a documented debit/credit fixture and reversal policy before enabling the UI action. No posting rule is inferred from an icon or status label.
For every PM item: validate balanced functional currency, account eligibility, period lock, event uniqueness, company scope, original-document linkage, maker-checker and atomic status+ledger transaction. Maintain test fixtures for original event, retry, reversal, concurrency, rounding and reporting reconciliation.
